    Guided trip setup, and stop claiming a bare trip is ready to review (#1822)
    
    * fix(trip-setup): guided inputs with inline help, and honest readiness
    
    A trip created with nothing but a title reported "Your trip plan is ready to
    review." and claimed "2 saved scenario draft(s)". Both were false: placeholder
    inventory and scenario drafts are generated for every trip the moment it is
    created, and readiness was derived from those generated artefacts rather than
    from anything the traveller supplied.
    
    Readiness is now derived from traveller-supplied context. Until the trip has a
    destination, travel dates, and (for business trips) a purpose for the approver,
    the workspace names exactly what is missing instead of claiming a reviewable
    plan, and generated drafts are not reported as decisions.
    
    The creation form was a bare list of unexplained inputs. It now:
    - makes trip type an explicit choice with consequences spelled out, rather than
      silently defaulting to Leisure in a product whose purpose is approval
    - explains every field inline: why it is needed and how it is used
    - derives trip length from the travel dates, and says so
    - rejects an end date before the start date instead of posting it
    - adapts the purpose field for business trips ("Approvers read this first")
    - tells the traveller what is still missing, and what happens after submission
    
    Test gates:
    - tests/app/test_workspace_readiness.py — a bare trip is not reported ready,
      generated artefacts are not claimed as decisions, business trips require a
      purpose, and a complete trip is still ready. Verified by deliberately removing
      the gate: 3 of 5 fail, then pass again once restored.
    - frontend/src/routes/NewTripPage.test.tsx — trip type cannot default silently,
      duration derives from dates, an inverted range blocks submission, and every
      field carries inline help.
    
    372 backend and 159 frontend tests pass.

    * fix(workspace): stop presenting generated placeholders as a finished plan
    
    The first screen after setup told the traveller their plan was "ready to review",
    listed "2 saved scenario draft(s)" and "1 inventory bundle(s) assembled" under
    DECIDED, and pointed them at a scenario comparison — when they had done nothing
    but type setup details.
    
    None of that was real work:
    
    - Route options 2 and 3 are the base option plus fixed arithmetic
      (_ensure_route_option_search_depth: +120.00/+240.00 cost, +45/+90 minutes,
      +1/+2 transfers).
    - The base cost is flat constants that ignore the destination entirely
      (inventory.py: lodging = days x $230 business, transport = $165, activities
      = $75, transport duration hard-coded to 45 minutes).
    
    Verified: trips to Reykjavik and Nairobi both return $1,160 / $1,280 / $1,400
    with identical travel minutes and transfers. A flight to Nairobi is priced the
    same as a cab across Chicago, and it was labelled "Estimated total" on a screen
    whose purpose is to produce an employer approval request.
    
    Changes:
    - Auto-generated drafts and bundles are never reported as traveller decisions.
    - The post-setup headline says what is true: "Trip setup is saved. Nothing has
      been planned yet.", and the next action describes the options as indicative
      route shapes rather than a plan to review.
    - The route tradeoffs panel carries an explicit notice that the figures are flat
      per-day rates that do not vary by destination and must not be used for a budget
      or an approval request.
    - "Estimated total" is relabelled "Indicative cost".
    
    This does not make the numbers real. It stops the product asserting they are.
